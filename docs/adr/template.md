# ADR-XXXX: <Title — a short noun phrase describing the decision>

**Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-YYYY | Deprecated
**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces — PRD requirements, existing
architecture, team constraints — shape this decision? Write this section
neutrally enough that someone who disagrees with the eventual decision
would still recognize it as a fair statement of the problem.

## Decision

What we decided, stated as a clear, active sentence: "We will use X for Y."

## Alternatives considered

What else did we look at, and specifically why didn't we pick it? This is
usually the section a future reader values most — it answers "did we think
about Z?" before they have to ask or, worse, re-litigate it.

## Consequences

What does this decision make easier? What does it make harder, or commit us
to? List the real tradeoffs — an ADR that only states upsides isn't
trustworthy, and won't help anyone reconsider the decision later if
circumstances change.
